High and Severe counties: what to do

Tana River Severe

Severe flood risk in Tana River

Very heavy rain of about 99 mm is expected on Thursday, with 209 mm over three days, and the Tana River is forecast to rise to about 3.3 times its recent level. Serious flooding and landslides are possible. Areas most at risk: Garsen, Tana Delta.

What to do

  • If you live near a river, on a steep slope or in a low-lying area, move to higher ground early. Don't wait until dark
  • Never walk, swim or drive through flood water
  • Keep children and livestock away from rivers and drainage channels
  • In an emergency call 999 or 112, or 1199 for the Kenya Red Cross
